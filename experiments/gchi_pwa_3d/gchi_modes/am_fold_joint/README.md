# Joint AM PWA cycle / fold fit: preliminary

This explores a fold penalty jointly with full-period RK4 trajectory, calcium extrema and short-segment losses, starting from `candidate_stable.json` in `am_fold_experiment`. The three full-state equilibrium anchors are algebraically pinned and the calcium knots held fixed. The implementation uses a clipped RK4 training proxy and soft spectral and physical constraints: validate independently. At each loss call it uses the affine first-return map and a soft determinant penalty for an additional unit multiplier, not a hard fold constraint. Full-period closure at reference period is not proof of an actual cycle.

The initially best joint candidate (`joint_refined.json`) has 12/12 detected cycles in `joint_refined_validation.json` with nominally held-out gamma values, starting at reference orbit states, DOP853 duration 1000 s. Conditional errors Cmin 0.02303, Cmax 0.02100, Imin 0.00997, Imax 0.00592 uM and period 0.41553 s. Fixed-point residual <=4.8e-16 and signs stable/stable/unstable/stable. The original AFM candidate had Cmin 0.01520, Cmax 0.01293, period 0.36099 s. These inputs were not held out with respect to the original baseline model selection and should not be treated as unseen experiments.

Do NOT claim a fold from the `section_C=.2` determinant: on this candidate the crossing speed is approximately 0.0022 and the section is close to the orbit's minimum C. The same seed transported to section C=.25 has crossing speed .0885, closure 2.41e-5 and det(DP-I)=-0.01625, independently confirmed by adaptive integration. The small determinant at .2 is not a reliable fold check for a not-exactly-closed orbit near tangency.

The alternative `joint_section25.json` has a near-grazing pair of crossings of the second calcium threshold, speed about +/-0.000486; although the determinant on one finite scan is close to zero, it is not proof of a nondegenerate fold. The `joint_nograzing.json` attempt kept the cycle farther from that threshold but the lowest regional eigenvalue became positive and det(DP-I) approximately -0.00244. Neither is selected as a verified model. `joint_nograzing` is not independently validated over the 12 cycles.

Reproduce from `gchi_modes`: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python fit_am_fold_cycles_local.py --data results/AFM/reference.json --start am_fold_experiment/candidate_stable.json --fold-seed am_fold_experiment/stable_candidate.json --c1 0.11277377008786005 --nfev 25 --output /tmp/joint_candidate.json`. Later fits use saved `joint_fold_seed.json` or `section25_seed.json` and `--section .25` as appropriate. Since optimization and grazing are sensitive to solver tolerances the saved outputs, not exact bitwise reproduction, are the evidence. Validate with `validate_modes.py` and independent Poincare first-return checks. Never overwrite source candidates.
